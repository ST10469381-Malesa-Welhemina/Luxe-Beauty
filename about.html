Our Story

Luxe Beauty was founded in 2024 by Malesa Walmena, a passionate entrepreneur in her early twenties. Inspired by her love for cosmetics and skincare, she wanted to create a beauty brand that helps people express themselves with confidence while offering affordable luxury.

Starting as a small online beauty store, Luxe Beauty has grown into a trusted destination for makeup, skincare, and beauty essentials.

Mission

To provide high quality beauty products that inspire confidence, celebrate diversity, and promote healthy skincare for everyone.

Vision

To become South Africa's most trusted beauty and cosmetics store by delivering quality products and exceptional customer experiences.

Objectives

  • Expand across South Africa.
  • Offer premium beauty products at affordable prices.
  • Deliver excellent customer service.
  • Promote self confidence through beauty.
  • Build long term customer relationships.

Core Values

  • + – Excellence
